What changes

You paid for a smart home. Nadya makes it one.

Most villas in Cyprus have tens of thousands of euros of automation and four apps nobody opens. Nadya turns all of it into one voice that knows your household.

Voice

Talk, don't command

Natural conversation in your language. She follows context and interruptions the way a person would.

Memory

Knows your family

Routines, preferences, who wakes when, what you asked last week. Remembers facts, never recordings.

Home

Runs the house

Lights, climate, shades, gate, cameras, pool and multiroom audio through the system you already have.

Face

On every screen

A live video avatar on your TV or wall panel when you want her. Voice-only the rest of the time.

A Thursday with Nadya

One day in a villa in Tala

07:15"Morning, Sasha."

Shades open slowly in her room, the kitchen is at 22°, school bag reminder on the hall panel.

09:30"Pool service at 11."

She lets the technician in, turns the camera feed off the kids' rooms, logs the visit.

13:00"You're flying Friday."

Holiday mode ready: lights on a lived-in schedule, AC off, irrigation kept.

19:40"Dinner outside?"

Terrace scene, music, pool heating. One sentence instead of three apps.

23:00"Goodnight."

Doors checked, alarm armed, the house goes quiet. She reports anything left open.

More familiar with time.
More helpful with every step.

A personal assistant can evolve alongside you. Nadya is designed to retain the context you choose and, over time, gain new ways to converse and help around the home.

Nadya.

Favourite music, daily routines and the things that matter to you. You decide what she remembers and what she forgets. More agreed context for a more personal conversation.

More expressive voice, new routines and new ways to interact. New features are introduced after compatibility checks for your home, and at your choice.

Privacy by design

A house that listens should forget on command.

Nadya is built by the team behind Mg Accounting, a Cyprus firm that handles company, tax and GDPR matters for the same families who buy these villas.

Audio

Wakes on her name. Raw audio is never stored.

Memory

Stored as facts, in the EU. "Nadya, forget this" deletes it.

Locks

Gate and doors need voice plus PIN. You set who can do what.

Log

Every action she takes is written to a log you can read.

AI Act

She always says she is an AI. No emotion recognition.
