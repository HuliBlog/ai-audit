Section 02

About the bank

The bank operates in the Astana International Financial Centre. The centre has its own regulator and its own law, separate from general Kazakh law. That is why the bank can take on non-resident companies remotely.

  • Full banking licence, not a payment agent
  • Islamic banking standards and a Shariah board
  • Multi-currency account: AED, CNY, EUR, USD and other currencies
  • Correspondent banks in the UAE, China, Europe, Turkey and the Gulf
  • Online banking and a personal manager for every client
  • Escrow for sale and purchase deals
The limits, upfront. The bank does not issue Visa or Mastercard cards. This is an account for transfers and holding funds, not for everyday spending. The currencies and routes available depend on your citizenship and residency, so we check them at the preliminary stage and tell you before you pay.

Section 06

Who it suits and who it does not

We take on
  • Trade and purchasing in China, Hong Kong, the UAE, Turkey
  • Companies from the UAE, Seychelles, Hong Kong, Cyprus, BVI and other jurisdictions
  • Settlements in dirhams and yuan
  • A working account without travel or local residency
  • Service, IT and consulting companies with international counterparties
  • Individuals who need an account outside their country, with a transparent source of funds
We do not take on
  • Companies registered in Russia: the bank does not onboard them
  • Companies from Kyrgyzstan: correspondent banks have closed this route
  • Companies resident in Kazakhstan and settlements in tenge: a restriction of the AIFC regulator
  • Gambling, betting, alcohol, adult: the bank holds an Islamic licence
  • Sanctioned persons and companies, payments from sanctioned banks
  • Certain countries and payment routes where the bank has restrictions
  • A card for everyday spending: the bank has no cards
We do not promise what is not ours to decide. The decision to open the account is the bank's. Our job is for you to learn the bank's position on your case before you pay, not after, and for the documents to reach compliance complete and consistent. Compliance takes the final decision on the full file, and the preliminary opinion does not replace it. So the rule is simple: if the bank does not open the account, you get the whole payment back.
