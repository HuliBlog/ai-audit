02 The problem

Restaurants create documents faster than bookkeeping can process them

The flow

  • Photos of receipts
  • PDF supplier invoices
  • Daily Z reports from the POS
  • Delivery platform statements
  • Card terminal batches
  • Handwritten cash expenses

The manual work

  • Identify the location
  • Find the supplier
  • Pick the account and category
  • Split the sales tax / VAT
  • Fill in the memo
  • Check the totals
  • Re-type it into QuickBooks

The owner's risk

  • A document gets lost
  • An expense never reaches the P&L
  • A duplicate entry is created
  • Errors are found too late
  • No clear view by location
  • The process does not scale

Automatic only in a guarded lane
