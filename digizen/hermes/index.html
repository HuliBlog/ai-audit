How it connects

Works where your team already works

Channels

  • Telegram bot: text and voice, own profile per employee
  • WhatsApp via the official Business API (Meta verification and conversation fees paid by the client)
  • The engine also supports Slack, Discord, Signal and iMessage

CRM

  • Bitrix24: live connection in our own accounting group, read-only at the start
  • KeyCRM connector quoted for the furniture case: clients, deals, saving quotes to the client card
  • Other systems through a connector or MCP

Documents and knowledge

  • Price lists, catalogues, templates, sample quotes in one shared base
  • Update the price list once, it updates for everyone
  • Google mail and calendar through a ready skill

Access rights

  • The bot listens only to approved Telegram IDs
  • Each employee gets a separate profile; one role and one base for the team
  • Writing to the CRM is switched on only after the pilot, through human approval
Security and data ownership

Your data stays in your company

01

The company owns the base

Knowledge and skills belong to the company, not to a personal account. An employee leaves, the assistant stays.

02

Your server, your AI subscription

The agent runs on a server and an AI model subscription that the client pays for directly. You can change the model provider.

03

Out only after "yes"

Messages to clients, quotes and statuses are shown first and sent only after a person approves. A person in the chat can always take over.

04

Narrow by default

For client-facing agents we switch off terminal and file access. The engine keeps an action allow-list with approval history and has an emergency pause.

The Mg Digital agent platform is designed with a separate container per client. In our accounting group design, sensitive topics such as tax advice are routed to a human by rule.

From our own operation

We run Hermes ourselves, since September 2026

Two of our own Hermes agents have been in daily work since September. One of them collects leads from 52 chats. These are the problems we already solved, so your team does not hit them.

Time zone

The server runs on UTC: a 17:00 task arrived at 20:00. Fixed in settings.

Too many messages

15 messages and 22,700 characters a day. Fixed with a length limit and a "nothing to say, stay silent" rule.

API costs

$50 of API spend in 4 days. Fixed with a subscription or a spend limit, watched with usage reports.

Lost mail access

Google revoked access after 7 days in test mode. Fixed by publishing the app in Google Cloud.

Silent failure

The model went down and the agent went quiet. Fixed with a fallback model.

Voice

Voice messages came out garbled with the wrong language setting. Fixed with the speech language setting.

Case 1 · in rollout

A furniture showroom: quotes by the whole team

Situation

The showroom prepares quotes for property developers. The owner did them himself, with his own AI assistant loaded with prices, the furniture list, sales terms and templates. Goal: the team prepares quotes as well as he does.

What we do

  • Move his assistant into Hermes, not rebuild it: instructions, files, best dialogues
  • Own assistant for each of up to 5 employees, one shared base
  • Quote check before sending, approval by the responsible person, summary for the owner

How it is accepted

  • Parity test: the same 5 requests give quotes no worse than his assistant, the owner compares himself
  • Acceptance call: each employee builds one quote on a live request

Scope

  • Team package plus three modules: CRM connector, pricing rules table, WhatsApp Business API
  • Optional support after the included month
  • Prices for the package and each module are on the packages slide

in rollout Package agreed in November 2026; migration of the existing assistant in progress as of 14 Nov 2026. Floor-plan layout and CRM go as the next stage.
