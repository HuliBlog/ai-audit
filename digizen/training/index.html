the format

Practice with a laptop, not a lecture

01

Bring a laptop

Participants build on their own computers and their own accounts, step by step with the host. Their data stays with them.

02

Task, prompt, check

Each practice block is a task, a ready prompt to copy and a clear check of "done". Usually four blocks per session.

03

A result every time

Every session ends with a working system, not a "try this at home" list. Stuck on a step? Raise a hand, help comes on the spot.

Length: 1.5 to 2.5 hours per workshop. Group: 15 to 20 people in our sessions so far. Materials: the announcement page and slides with all prompts stay with participants, so they can repeat the steps at their own pace.

the method

Five layers of a personal AI system,
one model for hiring any digital employee

The foundation: five layers

  • 1Brain: the AI model (Claude Code)
  • 2Memory: a folder with projects, people, promises
  • 3Rules: an instruction file about you and your work
  • 4Hands: Telegram, WhatsApp, email connected
  • 5Screen: a dashboard with what needs attention

The hiring model

  • IRole: a job description for the agent
  • IIReport: a short brief with the main thing on top
  • IIIChannel: it comes to your messenger by itself
  • IVWorkday: it runs on a schedule, e.g. at 8:00
  • VAssignments: it takes tasks and brings results

Learn the model once, and the team can "hire" the next digital employee on its own: operations, finance, content.

From personal tools to digital employees

IV2.5 hours

First digital team: assistant + sales agent

One hiring model, run twice. The assistant goes through mail, messengers and calendar every morning and sends a brief: who needs attention, what decisions wait, what it takes on. The sales agent knows the product, qualifies, keeps every contact in the CRM and brings back leads that went quiet.

works afterTwo "employees": the morning brief already arrived in your messenger, plus a sales brief and a follow-up draft for a real client.
