The problem

Three countries, three sets of books, no whole picture

A company in the UAE, a holding in Cyprus, one more in Hong Kong or Kazakhstan. Each country has its own accounting, its own format, its own currency and its own accountant.

01

How much did the group earn?

Each company reports on its own. Nobody adds them up in one standard.

02

Where is the money?

Balances and flows sit in different systems, formats and currencies.

03

Where is the risk?

Transactions between the group's own companies are booked differently on each side, and nobody compares them.

How it works

Four steps from separate books to one report for the owner

01

Collect

Financial data from several jurisdictions comes into one place.

02

Standardize

Different accounting formats are brought to a single standard.

03

Find mismatches

AI compares the companies of the group with each other and flags what does not agree.

04

Report

The result is consolidated management reporting for the owner.

One example

The same payment, two different stories

A payment inside the group is booked as a loan in one country and as revenue in the other. Each set of books looks fine on its own. Only a side by side comparison shows the mismatch.

Without consolidation

The mismatch surfaces late: at an audit, at a bank review or at a tax filing.

With AI consolidation

The mismatch is flagged when the data is compared, and an accountant decides how to correct it.

AI finds and shows. The accounting decision stays with a person.
