Taxes

Corporate tax: 0% on the first AED 375,000 of profit per year, 9% above that. For small businesses with revenue up to AED 3 million a year the tax is zero: the relief applies to tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2029. A return must be filed in any case. VAT at 5% applies once turnover exceeds AED 375,000 over 12 months.

The 0% rate for free zone companies applies only to qualifying income and only when the conditions of the zone and the tax law are met. We check whether it fits your activity before registration.
