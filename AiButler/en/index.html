Today a smart home is apps and scenes

A three-million villa, and to close the gates you open a third app. You message the housekeeper through a translator. You only hear how mom is doing when you call.

Smart home today

  • No conversation

    The house executes commands but does not talk. Anything outside the scene means picking up the phone again.

  • No memory

    The system does not remember how you like the light in the evening, when the housekeeper arrives or that a guest avoids dairy.

  • No personality

    Five family members get exactly the same house. Roles, habits and languages are not distinguished.

  • Nothing for the guest

    A villa guest will not study a wall panel. They will ask a person - or ask no one.

With Mg Intelligence Home

  • Talk to it like a person: no apps, no magic phrases.

  • Remembers habits, people and agreements for years.

  • Everyone in the family gets their own butler: role, language, habits.

  • Guests ask the house in their own language and get an answer.

Your home data stays private

We separate the AI layer from your physical system and from other clients data. Here is exactly how.

EU-hosted infrastructure

Where applicable, data is processed and stored on servers in the EU.

Access permissions

Roles for family, staff and guests: each level sees and controls only what it should.

Action logs

Every action the butler takes in the house is recorded in a log you can access.

PIN verification for sensitive actions

Opening gates, disarming the alarm or changing security settings requires PIN confirmation.

Memory deletion controls

You can ask the butler to forget a specific person, a conversation, or the whole house.

Raw audio handling

Voice is processed to generate a reply and, with your consent, to improve memory. Raw recordings are not sold or used for advertising.

AI disclosure

The butler discloses that it is AI, not a person, when it is relevant - to guests, staff, on the phone.

GDPR-oriented architecture

Client data isolation, data minimisation and the right to delete are built into the architecture from the start.

FAQ

Do I need to replace my smart home?

No. Mg Intelligence Home runs on top of what is already installed, through an integration layer - we do not rebuild your system.

Do you sell devices?

No. We sell the AI butler, setup on your system, onboarding, support and updates. Devices and installation stay with you and your integrator: during the survey we tell you what already works.

How much does AI usage cost?

It is already included in the plan. Voice and chat are unlimited. Only the video butler has a limit: up to 30 minutes of video a day for the villa, 60 for the office, 90 for Villa + Office, and no meter in the full package. If the video limit is regularly exceeded, we warn you in advance and suggest the next plan.

How long is the launch?

A villa takes 30-45 days from signing to a working home. For the office and the full package we give the timeline after the audit.

Is Alex a man or a woman?

Your call. Alex works as a name for both: you pick a male or female voice and avatar look during setup.

Which languages are supported?

Several languages work inside one house at the same time: family, staff and guests each get their own.

How is the Founding Home Program different from a regular plan?

It is not a clearance sale: the first 5 clients get 30% off a package, direct access to the product team and priority on new integrations. In return they take part in the initial rollout and give an open testimonial.

What is not part of the product today?

Integration with KNX, Control4 and Crestron is not native or certified: the link goes through an integration layer and is verified on your system before signing. We hold no data-protection certifications yet; a GDPR-oriented architecture is a design principle, not a legal opinion. The hologram and a physical embodiment of Alex are a direction of development, not something we sell today.

Is there a discount for paying annually?

Yes: pay for support a year ahead and get 2 months free.
