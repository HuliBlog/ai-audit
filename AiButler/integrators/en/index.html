Technical architecture

Mg Intelligence Home talks to your system through an integration layer - it does not replace the control panel.

  1. 1Resident voice or chat
  2. 2Mg Intelligence Home AI layer: personality, memory, logic
  3. 3Integration layer: APIs and drivers
  4. 4Your system: KNX / Control4 / Crestron
  5. 5Lights, climate, blinds, gates, media

We read and send commands through your existing API or driver. We do not replace your control panel or rewrite your configuration.
