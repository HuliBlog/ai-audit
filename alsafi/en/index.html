Section 01

About the bank

Al Safi Bank Ltd operates in the Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC). The centre has its own regulator and its own law, separate from general Kazakh law. That is why the bank takes on non-resident companies remotely.

Full nameAl Safi Bank Ltd
StatusThe first Islamic bank in the AIFC
LicenceAFSA-A-LA-2022-0026 dated 19 December 2022, Astana Financial Services Authority
CapitalUSD 12,375,000
OfficesAstana and Almaty
StandardsIslamic banking under AAOIFI standards, supervised by an independent Shariah board
What an Islamic bank means in practice. Any person and any company can be a client, religion does not matter. The difference is in the rules: the bank does not work with interest in the usual sense and does not serve certain businesses, such as gambling and alcohol. Every product goes through the Shariah board.

Section 05

Which documents are needed

The list follows the bank's checklist. We assemble the file with you and check it before submission, so compliance does not send documents back.

Company

  • Certificate of incorporation
  • Articles and incorporation documents
  • Proof of the company's address or a lease agreement
  • Document appointing the director
  • Share register no older than 6 months
  • Ownership structure, if a shareholder is itself a company
  • Passports of founders and shareholders
  • Licences and activity-related documents, where required

Individual

  • Passport
  • Proof of residential address
  • Proof of source of funds

What proves the source of funds

  • Property sale and purchase agreement
  • Bank statement
  • Tax return
  • Salary certificate

The bank's questionnaire

  • Source of funds and source of wealth are described separately
  • A section on politically exposed persons
  • Business activity, currencies and countries of payments
  • If a representative applies: a power of attorney and their passport
Where files are most often sent back. A share register older than six months and incomplete proof of source of funds. For a company the bank looks at how the capital of the company itself and of its owner was formed. We prepare these two points first.
Section 06

Limits worth knowing in advance

Who it suits
  • Trade and purchasing in China, Hong Kong, the UAE, Turkey
  • Companies from the UAE, Seychelles, Hong Kong, Cyprus, BVI and other jurisdictions
  • Settlements in dirhams and yuan
  • Service, IT and consulting companies with international counterparties
  • Individuals with a transparent source of funds
  • Sale and purchase deals through escrow
What the bank does not do
  • Does not open accounts for companies registered in Russia
  • Does not serve companies from Kyrgyzstan
  • Does not offer regular accounts to residents of Kazakhstan and does not convert tenge: a restriction of the AIFC regulator
  • Does not serve gambling, betting, alcohol, adult
  • Does not deal with sanctioned persons, companies or banks
  • Does not issue Visa or Mastercard cards
  • Has no mobile app, only online banking in the browser
  • Does not accept cash
Currencies and routes depend on you. The currencies and routes available are determined by the owners' citizenship and residency and by where payments come from and go to. So before submission we check your particular route and get the bank's preliminary opinion.
