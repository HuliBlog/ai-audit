How it works

Mg Intelligence Home fits into the existing chain - it does not replace anyone in it.

  1. 1The developer defines the residences and the handover schedule
  2. 2The existing integrator designs and installs KNX / Control4 / Crestron as usual
  3. 3Mg Intelligence Home connects on top as an AI layer: voice, memory, concierge
  4. 4The resident gets a personal butler from day one

Mg Intelligence Home does not replace the integrator and does not take over installation or maintenance of the automation system.
