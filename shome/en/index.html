Today a smart home is apps and scenes

The hardware got smart. The interaction did not.

No conversation

The house executes commands but does not talk. Anything outside the scene means picking up the phone again.

No memory

The system does not remember how you like the light in the evening, when the housekeeper arrives or that a guest avoids dairy.

No personality

Five family members get exactly the same house. Roles, habits and languages are not distinguished.

Nothing for the guest

A villa guest will not study a wall panel. They will ask a person - or ask no one.

Your home data stays private

We separate the AI layer from your physical system and from other clients data. Here is exactly how.

EU-hosted infrastructure

Where applicable, data is processed and stored on servers in the EU.

Access permissions

Roles for family, staff and guests: each level sees and controls only what it should.

Action logs

Every action the agent takes in the house is recorded in a log you can access.

PIN verification for sensitive actions

Opening gates, disarming the alarm or changing security settings requires PIN confirmation.

Memory deletion controls

You can ask the agent to forget a specific person, a conversation, or the whole house.

Raw audio handling

Voice is processed to generate a reply and, with your consent, to improve memory. Raw recordings are not sold or used for advertising.

AI disclosure

The agent discloses that it is AI, not a person, when it is relevant - to guests, staff, on the phone.

GDPR-oriented architecture

Client data isolation, data minimisation and the right to delete are built into the architecture from the start.

We do not claim certifications we do not hold. A GDPR-oriented architecture is a design principle, not a legal opinion.

FAQ

Do I need to replace my smart home?

No. Mg Intelligence Home runs on top of what is already installed, through an integration layer - we do not rebuild your system. A microphone, speaker or screen is sometimes added if you do not already have one. If there is no smart home at all, the Villa plan includes speakers or a screen at cost.

How much does AI usage cost?

It is already included in the plan. Voice and chat are unlimited. Only the video butler has a limit: up to 10 minutes of video a day for the personal agent, 30 for the villa, 60 for the office, and no meter in the full package. If the video limit is regularly exceeded, we warn you in advance and suggest the next plan.

What if it does not fit?

The Villa plan comes with a 45-day money-back guarantee if the agreed solution does not work.

How long is the launch?

A villa takes 30-45 days from signing to a working home. For the office and the full package we give the timeline after the audit.

Which languages are supported?

Several languages work inside one house at the same time: family, staff and guests each get their own.

How is the Founding Home Program different from a regular plan?

It is not a clearance sale: the first 3 villas in Cyprus get setup at €8 900 instead of €12 900, direct access to the product team and priority on new integrations. In return they take part in the initial rollout and give an open testimonial.

Is there a discount for paying annually?

Yes: pay the subscription for a year and get 2 months free.
